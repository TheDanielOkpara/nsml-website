# NSML CMS — Product Context

## Register
**Product.** The authenticated admin panel where NSML staff manage the public site's content. Design serves the task; the tool should disappear into the work.

## Users & purpose
- **Who:** 1–3 non-technical NSML team members (comms/admin staff). Not developers.
- **What they do:** publish/edit blog posts, manage property pages and their galleries, edit the team roster, read contact-form messages, and export newsletter subscribers.
- **Frequency:** intermittent — they log in to make a change, not to live in a dashboard. Speed-to-task and clarity matter more than analytics.

## Outcome
A staffer can log in, find the thing they need to change, change it, and confirm it's live — without second-guessing any control. Familiarity is the goal, not novelty.

## Brand anchors (identity preservation)
The public site is navy `#0d1f3c` + green `#1f9d55` on Syne/Outfit. The CMS keeps navy + green as anchors but executes in a calm **product** idiom (Inter, fixed scale, restrained accent), not the expressive brand idiom of the marketing site.

## Anti-references (what to avoid)
- Vanity dashboards: big-number stat-card grids that don't drive an action.
- Pill-shaped action buttons and loud full-saturation fills on every control.
- Generic "branded bootstrap admin" feel.

## Design principles
1. Restrained color: accent for primary action, current selection, and state only.
2. One type family (Inter), fixed rem scale, hierarchy by weight + size.
3. Every control has default/hover/focus/active/disabled. Empty states teach.
4. The dashboard surfaces work to do (unread messages, recent posts), not metrics for their own sake.
